# Code of Conduct

This project should be a welcoming place for people trying to build camera experiences in React.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent when discussing bugs or design tradeoffs.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Share enough detail for others to reproduce browser and device-specific behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive comments toward people reporting bugs.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that makes the project less welcoming, please open a private security advisory or
contact the maintainer through the repository owner profile.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block users when needed to keep the project
healthy.
